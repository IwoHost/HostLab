How Does This All Work?
🏠
Network Address
Think of this as the name of your street. It identifies the network itself, but no device actually gets this address. It's the starting boundary.
🚪
Default Gateway
This is the front door to the internet. Usually the first usable IP. Your router gets this address — all traffic going outside the LAN passes through here.
💻
Usable Hosts
These are the addresses you assign to your devices — computers, phones, printers, cameras. Anything that needs a network identity gets one of these.
📢
Broadcast Address
A message to everyone on the network at once. When data is sent here, all devices receive it. It's the last address in the range and can't be assigned to a device.
